- The blood-filtering portions of the kidney
- Consist of the [[Bowman's Capsule]] and [[glomerulus]]
#### Definition Breakdown
- "[[Renal]]" means *relating to the kidney*
- "[[Corpuscle]]" is a term for a *minute particle*
	- Derived from latin term "*corpusculum*", which means *small body*
		- You might be familiar with the Latin *corpus*, which means *body*
