- Network of [[Capillaries|capillaries]] located at the beginning of each [[Nephrons|nephron]]
- Receives blood supply from the [[afferent arteriole]] and blood exits out [[efferent arterioles]]
	- This is different than most organs, where the blood exits out of [[venules]]
