- Capillaries around the [[Nephrons|nephron]], specifically the **Loop of Henle**, in the **medulla** that run *parallel* to the tubules
#### Definition Breakdown
- "[[Vasa]]" comes from the Latin root *vās*, which means **vessels**
- "[[Recta]]" comes from *rēctus*, which means straight
- The term "*straight vessel*" should give you a hint into the difference between the **vasa recta** and the **[[peritubular capillaries]]**
	- **Peritubular capillaries** are more *web-like*
