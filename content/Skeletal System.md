- axial skeleton has skull and vertebrae

## Bone Composition
- made of #Mesoderm 

### Types of Bone
- [[Compact bone]] is super strong
	- Dense and strong
	- [[Bone matrix]]
		- Organic: [[Collagen]], [[glycoproteins]], [[peptides]]
		- Calcium, phosphate, and hydroxide ions
			- [[Hydroxyapatite]] crystals
		- Structural units called [[osteons]] or [[Haversian canals]]
			- [[Lamellae]] concentric circles of bony matrix
			- Longitudinal [[Haversian canals]] 
			- transverse [[Volkmann's canals]]
				- space = [[lacunae]] 
- [[Spongy bone]] also called [[cancellous bone]]
	- has bone spicules called [[trabeculae]]
	- Cavities between with [[bone marrow]]
		- Has [[red marrow]]
			- FIlled with [[hematopoietic stem cells]] 
		- [[Yellow marrow]] are made of fat
- [[Long bones]]
	-  [[appendicular skeleton]]
	- [[diaphyses]] that swell into [[metaphyses]] and ends in [[epiphysis]]
	- Outer is [[compact bone]] and inside is [[spongy bone]]
	- End is [[epiphyseal growth plate]]
		- [[Mitosis|mitotic cells]]
	- Surrounded by [[periosteum]]
