- The nature of fluid to flow down the osmotic potential gradient
	- So water wants to move from the hypotonic solution to the hypertonic solution

## Osmotic Potential Gradient
- Water wants to move from an area with higher concentration of water (low solute concentration) to lower concentration of water (high solute concentration)
