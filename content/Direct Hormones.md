- [[Hormone]] that acts on the target tissue and causes change in activity
- Example:- [[Triiodothyronine (T3)]] and [[Thyroxine (T4)]] are _direct hormones_ because they act **directly on tissues throughout the body** to increase metabolism, heart rate, temperature, etc.
	- [[Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH)]] is a [[Tropic Hormones|tropic hormone]] because it acts on another endocrine gland (the [[Thyroid|thyroid]]) to _stimulate_ the release of T3 and T4, rather than acting on body tissues itself.

*See [[HPT Axis]] for more information on the thyroid axis*
