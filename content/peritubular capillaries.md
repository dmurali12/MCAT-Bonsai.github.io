- [[Capillaries]] located right next to the nephron tubules

#### Definition Breakdown
- "[[Peri]]" means [*around, near, or surrounding*](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/peri)
- "Tubule", in this case, refers to the *tubular* structures of the [[Nephrons|nephron]]
- [[Capillaries]] are the small blood vessels that branch off an [[artery]] or [[vein]] to allow for *gas or fluid exchange* between the blood vessels and the other cells/tissues in our bodies
