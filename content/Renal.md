- Of or relating to the **kidneys**
	- Somewhat weirdly, however, physicians who specialize in kidney function are called **nephrologists**
		- "Nephros" in Greek means "kidney", but it seems closely related to the **[[Nephrons|nephron]]**, which are the functional units of the kidney
